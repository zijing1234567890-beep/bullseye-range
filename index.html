Bullseye Range

A first-person shooting gallery

You are the shooter on the range with three guns and a katana. Knock the targets down, drop the flags, and put down the shooters who fire back at you.

Drop a shooter and he may leave a glowing energy cell where he fell — it drifts into your hands on its own. Three cells load the energy pistols (key 5) and for the rest of the round they fire without ever running out. The cells do not carry over: each round you earn them again.

The same bargain buys the sniper rifle (key 6) — only the shooters leave cartridge rounds instead of cells. Three rounds load it; right click puts your eye to the scope.

Hit every quota to move on to the next level. Higher levels add more—and faster— targets, flags and gunmen. Take a round and your health drops; F patches you up with a med kit. The katana cuts them down if you get close enough, and if you right click to raise the guard it turns the rounds they fire back on them—hit a shooter with his own bullet and he goes down. Swing the blade as a round reaches you instead and it slices it clean in two.

  • Left click fire — hold it down to keep firing
  • Right click aim down the sights (with the katana: raise the guard and send their rounds back at them)
