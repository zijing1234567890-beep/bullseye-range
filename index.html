Bullseye Range

A first-person shooting gallery

You are the shooter on the range, with a rack of guns, a katana, a flamethrower and a fire extinguisher. Knock the targets down, drop the flags, and put down the shooters who fire back at you, and the men with knives who walk at you.

Drop a shooter and he may leave a glowing energy cell where he fell — it drifts into your hands on its own. Three cells load the energy pistols (key 5) and for the rest of the round they fire without ever running out. The cells do not carry over: each round you earn them again.

The same bargain buys the sniper rifle (key 6) — only the shooters leave cartridge rounds instead of cells. Three rounds load it; right click puts your eye to the scope.

And then there are the men with knives: no pistol, no cover, and no intention of stopping. They walk you down and cut you at arm's length — the blade coming up over the shoulder is the only warning you get. Put them down with anything in the rack, or take the flamethrower (key 7): its jet sets whatever it washes over alight, and a burning man drops where he stands. A raised katana turns his blade, too.

Out on the range drum fires are burning in the grass, and nothing in the rack will put them out — lead and flame only feed them. Take the fire extinguisher (key 8) and hold the blast on a drum until it hisses out: the crosshair goes foam-blue when the blast is on one, and the flames shrink back a little with every burst. A round is not over while a fire is still burning, so put out every one of them. The blast also chokes a shooter: with foam in his face he cannot draw a bead on you for a moment, which is often the moment you need.

Watch out for the men in high-vis coveralls with a red fuel can: they are not interested in you at all, and they walk to any drum you put out and set it going again. Kill them first — while one is still on his feet the fires will never stay down.

Put one of them down and the man with the tank comes for you: two red cylinders, a mask and a nozzle. He only starts walking when his mate goes down, and he walks slowly — but let him get close and he hoses you with burning fuel. The extinguisher's foam holds him up and stops him lighting it.

Clear a level and the range lays out three power-ups and buys you one of them — for the next round only, paid for out of your score. Click the one you want, or wave all three away and play the round as you are.

Hit every quota to move on to the next level. Higher levels add more—and faster— targets, flags and gunmen. Take a round and your health drops; F patches you up with a med kit. The katana cuts them down if you get close enough, and if you right click to raise the guard it turns the rounds they fire back on them—hit a shooter with his own bullet and he goes down. Swing the blade as a round reaches you instead and it slices it clean in two.
